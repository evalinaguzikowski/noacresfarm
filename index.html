About Us

No Acres Farm is a small, family-owned farm located in Pennington NJ. We specialize in growing a variety of fresh, organic produce that is available for purchase at our farm stand. Our commitment to sustainable farming practices ensures that our fruits and vegetables are not only delicious but also environmentally friendly. Come visit us and experience the taste of farm-fresh goodness!
